About TimeBackHQ

Find the few changes that give the owner room to lead.

TimeBackHQ helps owner-led businesses see where work is getting stuck, tighten the handoffs, and apply technology only where it creates practical leverage.

Start a conversationView the sample assessment

Built by an operator, not a tool reseller.

TimeBackHQ is led by David Schnaak, an Army Special Forces officer whose career has been spent finding practical asymmetric advantages and turning them into results under real constraints.

For an owner-led business, asymmetric advantage rarely means a big software project. It means seeing the operating picture sooner, recovering owner attention, tightening follow-up, and applying tools only where they create clear leverage.

That same habit shapes the work here: understand how the work actually moves, identify the real constraint, protect the judgment calls that only the owner can make, and implement only what helps the mission move forward.

The goal is not the loudest AI tool of the month. The job is to understand the repeated friction that steals time and remove it.

Discretion, stated plainly.

TimeBackHQ asks for the minimum context needed to understand a workflow and decide whether there is a fit. Sensitive client, patient, employee, legal, financial, government, password, or access information should stay out of the intake and fit conversation.

Military experience informs David's personal practice of discipline and discretion. It does not establish a legal confidentiality standard, cybersecurity control, or data-retention promise for TimeBackHQ.

If later work requires system access or sensitive information, the scope, access method, approvals, and boundaries must be agreed before that work begins.

Good fit looks like repeated friction, not a particular industry.

TimeBackHQ is not built around one industry or team size. The useful signals are operational.

  • The owner keeps getting pulled into routine coordination or context reconstruction
  • Important work crosses handoffs that are unclear or inconsistent
  • Follow-up, files, or decisions live across too many places
  • The business owns several tools but does not trust the operating rhythm
  • The team is willing to simplify the workflow before adding automation

Workflow comes before AI.

AI is useful only when it is attached to a real workflow. If the handoff is unclear, the intake is messy, or the decision owner is missing, another tool usually moves confusion faster.

TimeBackHQ looks first at where time and context are leaking. The answer may be a better process, a cleaner template, one visible queue, a bounded AI-assisted draft, or a separately scoped implementation.

Want a second set of eyes on the stuck work?

Start with the short intake. If there is a fit, David will schedule a no-cost 20 to 30 minute fit conversation (normally face-to-face or by phone) before any payment. The paid virtual assessment is a separate next step.
