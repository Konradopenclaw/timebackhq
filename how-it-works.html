Assessment process

A practical way to find where time is leaking.

The assessment is not a generic AI audit. It is a focused look at how work moves through the business, where it gets stuck, and which fixes are worth trying first.

Start a conversation Contact us

How the assessment works.

1

Audit

Map how work actually moves: intake, follow-up, files, handoffs, tools, invoices, communication, visibility gaps, and the admin that keeps pulling the owner back in.

2

Optimize

Simplify the workflow before adding tools. Clarify the handoff, remove repeated decisions, improve the template, and decide what should stay human-owned.

3

Amplify

Apply AI, automation, and SaaS only where they create measurable leverage: time returned, better follow-up, clearer visibility, lower risk, or less owner drag.

4

Leave with a clear next move

The final output is a short written assessment with a quick-win roadmap, recommended stack where useful, and DIY, Guided Setup, or TimeBackHQ project options.

Principles that keep the assessment practical.

The work starts with how the business actually operates. Tools only matter when they reduce friction in a real workflow and leave the right judgment calls with the right people.

What that means in practice

  • Start with the business problem before choosing a tool
  • Keep humans responsible for judgment calls
  • Use AI, automation, and SaaS only where they fit the workflow
  • Prefer practical fixes your team can actually use

The point is practical movement.

The owner’s judgment stays in the loop. TimeBackHQ helps clear away repetitive work, make scattered information usable, and turn the assessment into the next practical move.
